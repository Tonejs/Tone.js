Tone.LFO and other signal sources output a raw range (usually -1 to 1). On a hardware modular synth, the CV input on a filter is often paired with an "amount" knob that scales the incoming signal before it reaches the cutoff — without it, the raw voltage could swing the filter far beyond a musically useful range. This example builds that same attenuator explicitly with a Tone.Gain before connecting the LFO to the filter's frequency Signal.
